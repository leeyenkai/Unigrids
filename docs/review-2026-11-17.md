# Code review and feature audit — 17 November 2026

A review of the app as it stood at `8c2b38d` (0.10.0 in progress: Japan and Norway).
Five read-only reviewers each took one area: Core, Reference and Log; the map, guide
and trail screens; passport, stats, trips and privacy; the Python pipeline; and a
feature and data audit across the three countries. Every finding below was traced
through the code. The fixes were then checked again, tested and committed. This file
records what was fixed and, more usefully, what is still open.

## Where the app stands

Japan's and Norway's national parks match a US park on everything the guide template
shows: outline, written guide, story, sights, chronology, safety and regulations,
climate, credited photographs, the 3D explorer, a passport slot and stats. They also have
what the US lacks: a local name, How this park works, and access lines for island and
Arctic parks. The differences that remain are mostly data the sources do not publish
(monthly visitation abroad; Norway's visitor numbers; Japanese trail grades), and a
few gaps the OpenStreetMap fetch left (below).

## Still open

### Data abroad (fetch, not code)

- **Japan: huts for 32 parks, trails for 8, and the main stations and Shinkansen lines**
  (Tokyo, Sapporo, Shin-Osaka, Hakata, Matsumoto). All wait on a full OpenStreetMap
  fetch. Overpass refused this machine partway, so a Geofabrik extract read
  locally, as the plan intended, is the way round it.
- **Campsites abroad**, Norway's park leaflets as PDFs, Norway's remaining 21 authorised
  visitor centres, 56 quasi-national park outlines, Japan's park zones and Norway's dated
  restriction areas on the guide map, and Turrutebasen's routes named only "Ukjent".
- Four Norwegian parks have no photographs (Anárjohka, Borgefjell, Dovre,
  Lomsdal-Visten).

### Pipeline

| Where | Finding |
|---|---|
| `countries/common.py` `latest_raw` | A half-fetched snapshot is read as complete; `_NPSData.load` returns `[]` for a missing file. A `--skip-fetch` build after a failed fetch ships partial data silently |
| `lands_build.py`, `state_parks.py` | Registry keys are built from names after cleaning, not from the sources' own IDs (`ORGCODE`, `NLCS_ID`), so a rename or a cleaning change orphans logged visits |
| `trail_catalogue.py`, `abroad/trails.py` | A 0.02° buffer puts the same trail in two parks (9 in both Sequoia and Kings Canyon), and a route clipped to a park keeps its end-to-end name |
| `abroad/trails.py`, guides | Keys abroad rest on the lowest OSM way ID or a sight's display name |
| `lands_build.py` | Alaska Maritime refuge's islands east of 180° (Attu, Kiska, Amchitka) are dropped |
| `us/fetching.py`, `brochures.py`, `trail_catalogue.py` | Transient failures cached as complete; brochure codes taken from the last build's places |
| `jp/fetching.py` | Wikidata rows keyed by Japanese label; several inception dates resolve to whichever came first |
| `jp/content.py`, `no/content.py` | A guide that fails to parse is treated as absent |
| `state_parks.py` | `sp-co-roxborough` beside `sp-roxborough`, and `sp-al-desoto-al` beside `sp-al-desoto`: two same-state duplicates with their own causes |
| `wikipedia_match.py` | About ten state parks are described by their reservoir or mountain (Navajo Lake, Mount Diablo); true of the place but not about the park. Kohler–Andrae's two halves have no lead |

### Not defects

- The pipeline's User-Agent names the app and its public repository, as MET Norway's
  terms ask. It carries nothing about any reader.
- Monthly visitation, Norway's visitor numbers and Japan's trail grades are absent
  because no source publishes them (`docs/sources-japan-norway.md`).
