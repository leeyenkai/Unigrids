# Sources for Japan and Norway: the Phase 0 check

Checked on **2026-11-16** for the 0.10.0 plan ("The idea that travelled"). Every row below
comes from the publisher's own page, endpoint or file, fetched that day, except where a
row or note says otherwise (a few items rest on a search result or a page summariser and
say so). "Re-checked" means the request was made again before this file was written.
Anything that could not be confirmed is marked **UNCONFIRMED** with the reason; nothing
was filled in from memory. Requests used generic client names and carried no details of
the owner or the project.

This file is the input to the pipeline's `SourceTerms` entries, the `NOTICE.md` rows and
the Attributions screen. The credit lines quoted here are the publishers' wording. Lines
marked "proposed" are built from a publisher's template and are not published by it.

## How to read it

- **Verdicts.** **USE**: take it as the plan says. **USE WITH CONDITIONS**: usable, and the
  conditions in the numbered note matter. **FALLBACK**: only if the primary fails.
  **REJECT**: do not use. **UNRESOLVED**: no answer yet; the row says who can give one.
- **Three tables**: Japan, Norway, and the rows both countries share. Each row's number
  (J, N, B) is what the notes refer to.
- The plan's Sources table has 17 rows; the crosswalk below gives each a verdict.

### Japan: notes behind the verdicts

- **J-1. The outline file is orphaned.** MOE's Web-GIS (`gis.biodic.go.jp`) no longer resolves in DNS ("自然環境調査Web-GISは2026年5月末でサービスを終了します"), and the live `nps_nwp_nca_cz.html` page lists no national park dataset; neither does the いきもの地図 table or 環境ジオポータル (`geoportal.env.go.jp`, 122 items, none a park or zone layer). The file itself is still served (`nps.zip`, re-checked today). Snapshot it into `Pipeline/raw/<date>/biodic/` with a `source.json` recording the date, byte size, `Last-Modified` and the archive URL, and ask MOE 自然環境局 国立公園課 (03-3581-3351, the contact the old page named) where the dataset lives now and where the quasi-national parks are. MOE's caveat, verbatim from the archived page: 「本データは、指定区域の概要を示すために作成したものであり、誤差を含みます。」 Never describe the outlines as legal boundaries. The DBF has no code, date or area: join to J1 by name (all 35 `NAME` values match the 35 names on env.go.jp, none extra). `ZONE` polygons: 第2種特別地域 4,860; 第1種特別地域 3,632; 特別保護地区 3,448; 第3種特別地域 1,627; 普通地域 1,504; 海域公園地区 218; 区分未定 4 (an "undecided" class beyond the plan's list). 68.9 MB unzipped, so simplify before bundling. The KML that existed in 2025 is no longer linked; its URL was not looked for.
- **J-2. No current national source for the 58 quasi-national parks.** A10's park names are empty (`OBJ_NAME` blank in the Nagano and Kyoto files opened) and `AREA_SIZE` is 0; its own page calls the special-zone subdivisions 参考表示の扱いであり、精度は保証できません. Against today's list its code table lacks 中央アルプス (2018), 御嶽山 (2026), 京都丹波高原 (2016) and 甑島 (2015) and still lists 日高山脈襟裳, 南三陸金華山 and 奄美大島, which have since become national parks (a diff of the spec's code table, not of a full download). MOE has no quasi-national dataset on biodic or 環境ジオポータル; MOE's ArcGIS portal (`pl-moej.gisservice.jp`) has a few office-level layers with no licence text; EADAS (`eadas.env.go.jp`) answers 403 to scripted clients, so any download and its terms are UNCONFIRMED; Toyama's open-data site was reported to offer 国定公園 (SHP, 2021-04-01) but could not be opened. **Ontakesan is a quasi-national park (designated 2026-04-10, 28,275 ha, Nagano and Gifu; 指定書 `https://www.env.go.jp/content/000391139.pdf`), not a national park.** MOE: "今回の指定によって、国定公園は全国で58公園となります" (`https://www.env.go.jp/press/press_03975.html`). It is the only one of the 58 newer than any dataset, so the plan's "newer than the data" path (OSM outline, else a point) applies.
- **J-3. GSI.** Tiles need attribution only: real-time loading from GSI's servers in a website or app needs no application (Q&A 1-12, `https://www.gsi.go.jp/LAW/2930-qa.html`). **GSI does not address pre-fetching tiles in a pipeline and bundling derived data (offline packs, hillshade) in a distributed app.** Section 2's wording is a general 利用 with no copy or redistribution carve-out, which is encouraging, but nothing says "bundling is fine": send GSI one email. Do **not** use the 基盤地図情報 downloads (the underlying DEM files): they are 基本測量成果 needing a 測量法 application (Q&A 2-4: a relief tinted from DEM10B combined with 電子地形図25000 needs a 第30条 use application). The 5 m and 1 m DEMs come from other bodies "作成機関の使用承認を得て提供", so per-source credit may be needed (UNCONFIRMED). No bulk-fetch rate limit found. Use `dem_png` (DEM10B) and `dem5a_png` for Chubu-Sangaku and Fuji; Copernicus GLO-30 is the fallback if GSI declines (tiles for Fuji, Shiretoko, Jotunheimen and Svalbard N78 E015 all answer HTTP 200 on the AWS bucket).
- **J-4. Grading tables.** Per-route facts cannot be taken in bulk on these pages' authority. Options: (a) written permission from each prefecture's 山岳 contact to reproduce route name and grade with a credit, which leaves a third-party dependency on tables that change yearly; (b) show the grade only as a link-out to the prefecture's PDF; (c) drop published grades for Japan and keep the Effort estimate. Participants (Nagano's hub, 令和6年4月): Niigata 113, Yamanashi 123, Nagano 123, Shizuoka 82, Gifu 75, Gunma 93, Tochigi 102, Yamagata 103, Akita 33, Toyama 92, plus Ishizuchi (50) and Sobo-Katamuki-Ozure (63).
- **J-5. Visitation.** The 令和6年 overview PDF: 35 national parks 3億4,007万人; 57 quasi-national parks at that date; 311 prefectural. Footnotes to carry: 日高山脈襟裳十勝 counts only from 2024-06-25; やんばる 令和5年 reused the 令和4年 figure because Okinawa did not report in time. The table is a multi-block print layout (several year ranges side by side, a name row plus a number row per park) and uses pre-merger names for older rows (霧島屋久, 西表, 利尻礼文 and サロベツ on separate lines), so the parser needs a name map and tests.
- **J-6. JMA.** `warning.json` drives JMA's own map (`https://www.jma.go.jp/bosai/map.html#contents=volcano`, which fetches `volcano_list.json`, `warning.json`, `eruption.json` and `info.json`). On 2026-11-16 it lists 12 volcanoes: レベル２（火口周辺規制）×6, レベル３（入山規制）×1 (桜島), レベル１ ×1 (雌阿寒岳, lowered 2026-11-04), 火口周辺危険 ×2, 周辺海域警戒 ×2. Entries carry `reportDatetime`, `eventId` (the volcano code in `volcano_list.json`), item `name`, numeric `code` and `condition` (継続/引上げ/引下げ/切替). **It is not documented as an API** (JMA: "コンテンツは予告なく変更、移転、削除等が行われることがあります"), and **no JMA document says what absence from the file means**: a volcano missing from it may be at 噴火予報 (Level 1), but show "no current warning listed by JMA", never "Level 1". Old entries persist (硫黄島 from 2007), so show `reportDatetime`. The documented Atom feeds only carry 噴火警報・予報 when a level changes (`eqvol_l.xml` held about seven days of 解説情報, 降灰予報 and 観測報 but no warning), so they cannot give the current level between changes. Feed rule: "1日10GB以上のダウンロードを伴うアクセスが確認された場合、アクセス元のIPアドレスを遮断します"; no minimum polling interval stated. Two statutes shape the panel (JMA 留意事項): 気象業務法第17条 (independent forecasts need a JMA permit; changing JMA's values or areas and re-supplying them counts) and 第23条 (only JMA may issue warnings: 「公開ＸＭＬ電文中の本質を損なうような編集は認められません」). JMA's permit FAQ says relaying its forecasts and warnings as-is does not count as a forecasting business. So: show JMA's own text and level name unchanged, name JMA, link to the JMA volcano page, derive no advice of our own. Decode leniently and hide the panel on failure.
- **J-7. Regions.** MOE's page groups parks into 7 地区 (北海道, 東北, 関東, 中部, 近畿, 中国四国, 九州). The offices are now **地方環境局** (北海道環境局, 東北環境局, 関東環境局, 中部環境局, 近畿環境局, 中国四国環境局, 九州環境局, plus a 四国事務所 inside 中国四国) and three **自然環境事務所**: 釧路 (知床, 阿寒摩周, 釧路湿原), 信越 (上信越高原, 妙高戸隠連山, 中部山岳), 沖縄奄美 (奄美群島, やんばる, 慶良間諸島, 西表石垣). ### Norway: notes behind the verdicts

- **N-1. `vernedato` is the regulation's date, not the founding date.** Rondane shows 2003-10-24 in the data, while the park page says «Rondane ble vernet som Norges første nasjonalpark i 1962.» Jotunheimen shows 2014-11-14 against «Verna i 1980». `foerstegangVernet` is empty for most parks (Forlandet has 1973-07-01). "Established" must be a curated per-park fact with its source. Rondane 1962 is confirmed from norgesnasjonalparker.no.
- **N-2. No official area field.** Computed geodesic area for Rondane is 969.3 km² against the published 963.5 km². Curate `areaHectares` from the park page, or label a geometry-derived value as approximate.
- **N-3. MET Norway's terms and "no backend".** The terms say «Browsers and mobile apps should not contact the API directly, but instead use a local proxy (backend for frontend) server», but «Low-volume websites and mobile apps may use simple cross-origin Javascript requests … provided the sites identifies itself as described above. If you start generating a lot of traffic against the API, you must set up a caching proxy gateway». Other rules: `User-Agent` with the app or domain name plus a company email address or website link (without contact details «you risk being blocked without warning»); keep total traffic under 20 requests per second; never repeat a request before `Expires`; send `If-Modified-Since`; at most one poll every 10 minutes; a mobile app «must not retrieve new data as long as the application is not in use». Land alerts are per county (`/locations` lists 15 mainland counties and 56 sea areas, no county 21); a lat/lon query at Longyearbyen returned 0 alerts, so there is no Svalbard land coverage.
- **N-4. Varsom regions.** `Region/0` returns 46 regions with polygons and county and municipality lists, so a park maps to a region from its outline. Svalbard: **3001 Svalbard øst (B), 3002 Svalbard vest (B), 3003 Nordenskiöld Land (A), 3004 Svalbard sør (B)**; Jotunheimen is 3028 (A). Language key 1 = no, 2 = en. Flood and landslide are keyed by county and municipality (`Warning/County/{no}/…`, `Warning/Municipality/{no}/…`); Svalbard is county 21 and municipality 2100 «Svalbard, Bjørnøya, Hopen» (park records say 2111, so map through county 21). `Warning/All` returned 718 rows for three days, mostly green level with placeholder ids `xxx`: filter on ActivityLevel above 1.
- **N-5. Restriction areas.** Scope is only Ferdselsforbud (traffic ban), Landingsforbud (landing) and Lavflyving forbudt (low flying below 300 m), plus Forsvaret variants. Dates are structured only in the GeoJSON, as a JSON string in `restriksjoner_detaljert`, e.g. `{"fra_dag":"1","fra_maaned":"5","til_dag":"31","til_maaned":"7","restriksjonstype":"FerdselForbud","merknad":""}`; the REST service has only text such as «Ferdselsforbud (1.5-31.7)». Parks with ferdselsforbud: Børgefjell (zone A, 20.6–25.7), Raet, Jomfruland, Ytre Hvaler and Færder (15.4–15.7), Van Mijenfjorden (15.5–15.8), and year-round sites in Nordvest-Spitsbergen and Sør-Spitsbergen. Landing and low-flying bans cover nearly every park. Of 651 ferdselsforbud polygons, 494 lie in nature reserves.
- **N-6. Local names.** `offisieltNavn` packs Norwegian, Sámi and Kven names into one string with «/» and irregular spacing («Varangerhalvøya nasjonalpark/Várnjárgga álbmotmeahcci/ Varenkinniemen kansalistaras»; «Anárjohka» has the Sámi form first). Language tags need hand curation, and Kven (`fkv`) joins nb, nn, se, smj, sma.
- **N-7. Counts on 2026-11-16 from the dataset.** 48 national parks (41 `Nasjonalpark` + 7 `NasjonalparkSvalbard`), 195 landskapsvernområder (135 plain + 60 combined forms), **2,752** naturreservater (2,729 mainland, 22 Svalbard, 1 Jan Mayen), 23 marine areas (Miljødirektoratet's page says 18 at the end of 2024), 3,514 records. Miljødirektoratet's page `https://www.miljodirektoratet.no/ansvarsomrader/vernet-natur/norges-verneomrader/` says «I 2024 hadde vi 48 nasjonalparker i Norge, inkludert Svalbard» and «195 landskapsvernområder». The plan's 2,714 matches nothing current. The Svalbard seven: Sassen-Bünsow Land, Indre Wijdefjorden, Forlandet, Nordvest-Spitsbergen, Nordre Isfjorden, Van Mijenfjorden, Sør-Spitsbergen.
- **N-8. Machine routes.** Use the blob Atom feeds and `kart.miljodirektoratet.no` (both HTTP 200). `geoapi.p.aks.miljodirektoratet.no` (the Geonorge cart link) and `kart3.miljodirektoratet.no` answer 403 to scripts. Recheck the Kartverket county record's "Arkivert" status before the pipeline pins it.
- **N-9. NLOD §5** (`https://data.norge.no/nlod/en/2.0`): attribute as the licensor specifies, refer to the licence, link where practical, and «If the information has been changed, the licensee must clearly indicate that changes have been made by the licensee». The Attributions screen therefore says the outlines were simplified by Unigrids. The notice may sit on an About page.
- **N-10. Svalbard rules for the "How this park works" panel.** The fork found only NPI's Cruise Handbook (last updated June 2015). The current rule is the Field Operation Safety Regulations (feltsikkerhetsforskriften), FOR-2025-06-06-940, `https://lovdata.no/dokument/SFE/forskrift/2025-06-06-940`: in force 2026-01-01 (sections 10–15 and 17 from 2027-07-01). As read from Lovdata through a page summariser, not word for word: visitors must notify the Governor at least four weeks before a trip outside the "travel area" (map in Appendix I) and may not start before they get a response (s. 7); a report follows within four weeks (s. 19); rescue insurance (s. 16, from 2026-01-01) with limits of NOK 120,000 / 350,000 / 650,000 by zone; alerting and two-way communication equipment (s. 17, from 2027-07-01). The Governor's guidelines for polar bear protection are separate. **Read the Lovdata text itself, and the Appendix I map, before writing the copy.** Visit Svalbard's page (undated) still describes the older "report trips beyond Management Area 10" wording.

### Both countries: notes behind the verdicts

- **B-1. Natural Earth's Norway layer is the pre-2018 county set**: 19 counties (Nord-Trøndelag, Sør-Trøndelag, Hedmark, Oppland, Sogn og Fjordane, Hordaland, Aust- and Vest-Agder, Oslo …) plus NO-21 Svalbard and Bouvet Island. It reflects neither the 2020 nor the 2024 reform. Norwegian counties must come from Kartverket (N5); Natural Earth is Japan-only.
- **B-2. Wikidata counts disagree with the official lists, so Wikidata can only supply QIDs and sitelinks.** Japan's national-park class `Q1071482` has 35 items (Shiretoko is `Q739391`). Quasi-national parks, `Q11832860`, have **60** items against MOE's 58. Norway has no country-specific class: Rondane (`Q955861`) is typed with the generic `Q46169` "national park" (2,566 items worldwide), and 51 of them have country Norway against the 48 official parks. Match by name against the official list; never select parks by class alone.
- **B-3. Geofabrik.** The Norway page does not say whether `norway-latest` includes Svalbard, and lists "Svalbard and Jan Mayen" separately: fetch that extract for Svalbard trails and huts, or check the polygon, when the fetcher is written. The plan's size estimate for Japan (about 2 GB) is 2.4 GB.
- **B-4. Open-Meteo.** Today's US build has the same exposure (`NOTICE.md`: "free for non-commercial use"). If 1.0 ships paid, with ads or with a subscription, the free API no longer applies: take a commercial plan, or change the forecast source (WeatherKit is already the planned alternative in `SPECIFICATION.md` §17). The pipeline's offline archive use for climate is a different use from live forecasts in the app and may be decided separately.
- **B-5. Wikimedia User-Agent policy** (`https://foundation.wikimedia.org/wiki/Policy:Wikimedia_Foundation_User-Agent_Policy`): a client must identify itself with contact information, `<client name>/<version> (<contact>) <library>/<version>`; generic agents may get HTTP 403. The pipeline's Wikidata, Wikipedia and Commons client needs a descriptive User-Agent with a project URL the owner picks. This applies to the offline pipeline, not the shipped app.
- **B-6. Copernicus GLO-30 is a surface model**: tree canopy and buildings are in the heights, so summits and forest trail profiles read high against a terrain model. Fine for hillshade and profiles, but label it ("modelled surface elevation") and never let it override an official summit height. A search result for the Copernicus Data Space page says its GLO-30 *view service* is restricted from 2026-07-28 (not re-read); the AWS bucket download is the route to use and is a different access path. GLO-30 "Public" omits tiles for a few countries; the four tiles checked above are present.

## What changes the plan

Ordered by how much work each one moves.

1. **Japan's national park outlines are on borrowed time.** Replace "MOE Biodiversity Center GIS … shape/KML" with `biodic.go.jp/shapedata/nps.zip`: Shapefile only, JGD2000, data of 2025-04-25, no longer linked from any MOE page and its Web-GIS closed in May 2026. The fetcher must snapshot it and record `Last-Modified`; add a standing task to ask MOE's 国立公園課 for the successor. The Risks row "MOE data source may disappear" is live (J-1).
2. **A10 is dropped; quasi-national outlines have no national source.** Use OSM first, cross-checked against MOE's 58 names, or prefectural open data where terms allow. **Ontakesan is a quasi-national park (designated 2026-04-10), not a national park**: fix the plan's "Ontakesan, 2026" wording. The counts 35 national and 58 quasi-national are right (J-2, J-8).
3. **Norway's "Established" and area cannot come from the outline dataset.** `vernedato` is the current regulation's date (Rondane 2003-10-24, against «vernet … i 1962»), and there is no official area field (computed Rondane area is 0.6% off the published figure). Curate both per park with the park page as source; keep Rondane 1962, whose sentence is quoted in N-1 (N-1, N-2).
4. **Norwegian counts to pin in tests**: 48 national parks (41 + 7 on Svalbard), 195 landskapsvernområder, **2,752** naturreservater (2,729 + 22 + 1). Drop 2,714, which matches nothing current (N-7). The plan's 83-park figure holds.
5. **MET Norway's terms collide with "no backend".** They say mobile apps «should not contact the API directly» and use a proxy, allow low-volume direct calls with an identifying User-Agent, and require a caching proxy at high volume. MetAlerts is county-level and has no Svalbard land coverage. Make **Varsom (NVE) the primary Norwegian warning source** (NLOD, no User-Agent rule, Svalbard avalanche regions 3001–3004), treat MetAlerts as optional, and record the decision in ADR 0015 (N-3, N-4).
6. **JMA volcano alert levels have an official current-state source after all**: `bosai/volcano/data/warning.json`. It is first-party but undocumented as an API, and absence from it means nothing JMA states. Rewrite the Risks row; show "no current warning listed by JMA", never "Level 1"; relay JMA's text unchanged, name JMA, derive no advice (Meteorological Business Act Arts. 17 and 23). Put that rule in ADR 0015 (J-6).
7. **Elevation.** Japan: GSI tiles are attribution-only and need no application; bundling derived data in an app is not addressed, so send GSI one email, and keep Copernicus GLO-30 as the fallback; avoid the 基盤地図情報 downloads (Survey Act). Norway: use Kartverket's WCS at 10 m (there is no current "DTM 10" download), and NPI's 20 m model for Svalbard (J-3, N7, N8, B-6).
8. **Photographs.** Norway: Miljødirektoratet and norgesnasjonalparker.no photos are off the table (no licence; the photographer holds the exclusive right), so Norwegian heroes come from Commons only and there will be gaps. Japan: the plan's credit form `「○○の写真」（環境省）` was not found on any MOE page; use `出典：環境省ホームページ （当該ページのURL）` plus any named photographer, and never the 素材集. The pipeline must carry PDL 1.0 as its own licence: `_wikimedia_image` accepts and marks public domain any licence name starting "pd" (`reference_build.py:87` and `:695`) and `_guide_image` marks every guide photo public domain (`reference_build.py:955`), so a "PDL 1.0" photo would pass as public domain if it went through either (J16, N21).
9. **Published trail grades.** Japan: the prefectural tables are PDF-only and "All Rights Reserved"; either get written permission per prefecture, link out, or drop them and keep the estimate (J-4). Norway: `gradering` exists, but the Oslo file carries a value on only 1,002 of 3,466 routes and mostly `I`; quote the codes, never the criteria text (N10).
10. **Visitation.** Use MOE's Excel (令和6年, published October 2026), not e-Stat (stale since 2018); the multi-block layout needs a purpose-built parser and a name map. Norway stays absent (J-5, J12).
11. **Svalbard is thin everywhere.** Kartverket's counties exclude it (derive NO-21 from park records), its terrain model is mainland-only, MetAlerts skips it, and flood and landslide warnings are keyed to municipality 2100 while park records say 2111. The only Svalbard visitor rules the Norway fork found were from 2015; the current rule is the Field Operation Safety Regulations, `FOR-2025-06-06-940`, in force 2026-01-01 (notify the Governor four weeks ahead for trips outside the "travel area"; N-10). Read the regulation itself before writing the "How this park works" copy. One authorised visitor centre is in Longyearbyen: check before exempting the Svalbard parks from visitor centres (N12).
12. **Seasonal restrictions are narrower than the plan says**: only traffic bans, landing and low flying, with structured dates only in the GeoJSON, and few parks carry a traffic ban (N-5).
13. **Japan's regions.** MOE's offices are now 地方環境局; the eight passport regions are Unigrids' own split and Setonaikai (spanning 近畿, 中国四国, 九州) needs a rule (J-7).
14. **Framing.** MOE's history page does not support "after its advocates had studied the American parks"; source that clause separately or drop it. The dates (1872, 1931, 1934-03-16, 1957, Rondane 1962) are confirmed (J-8).
15. **Open-Meteo is non-commercial only** (as in the US build). A paid, ad-supported or subscription release needs a commercial plan or another source before 1.0 (B-4).
16. **Machine endpoints.** Use the blob Atom feeds and `kart.miljodirektoratet.no`; the Geonorge cart link (`geoapi.p.aks.miljodirektoratet.no`) and `kart3.miljodirektoratet.no` answer 403 to scripts. The pipeline's Wikimedia client needs a descriptive User-Agent (B-5), and MET Norway's the app name plus a contact or project URL, which the owner picks once.

## The guides' fact-check (November 2026)

Every claim in the 83 guides was checked against the parks' authorities (MOE and its
regional offices, japan.travel, GSI's summit list of 31 March 2026, Miljødirektoratet,
Lovdata, Sysselmesteren, the Norwegian Polar Institute, Store norske leksikon) and
Wikipedia: about 6,150 claims, some 320 corrected or narrowed and 150 removed for want
of a source. Decisions taken, which keep the guides consistent:

- **Areas.** Norway's are the current regulation's figure on Lovdata, even where
  Miljødirektoratet's web page or the measured outline differs (Hardangervidda 3,422 km²
  against 3,445; Anárjohka 1,390 against 1,414; Rohkunborri 571 against 556; Rago 162
  against the page's 171, after its 2021 amendment). Svalbard's 1973 parks, whose
  regulations give no area, keep the Norwegian Polar Institute's. Japan's match MOE's
  national park table of 10 April 2026 for all 35.
- **Second-tier dates.** A Norwegian protected landscape or nature reserve shows the year
  it was first protected (`foerstegangVernet`) or none: `vernedato` is the current
  regulation's date, years later for an area re-regulated since (Utladalen: 1980, not
  2014). 151 of the 195 landscapes and 289 of the 415 reserves the pipeline reads have no first date.
- **Sights just outside a park** (about 20 in Norway: Sarafossen, Kittilbu, Nigardsbreen's
  foot) are kept as gateways, as in the US guides; no note claims they are inside.
